Tide

Sep 2026 · Marine species ID

Snap one photo of a sea creature and Tide identifies it, then tells you whether to keep it or let it go. Species ID runs through the Google Gemini API with a confidence score, checked against GBIF and the IUCN Red List. Four public APIs (GBIF, IUCN, NOAA, Wikimedia) feed its conservation cards and sustainable alternatives. Built with Issaka and Oliver.

ClearBill

Hospital bill analyzer

Hospital bills are full of mistakes. ClearBill reads every line, catches duplicate charges and math errors, and writes the dispute letter for you. On the sample bill above it scans 27 line items and flags four issues, including an epidural billed twice. Built full-stack with React, Python/Flask and SQL, with Stripe for secure payments and Firebase for authentication and data storage.
